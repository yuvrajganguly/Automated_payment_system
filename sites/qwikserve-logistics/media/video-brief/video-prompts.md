# Qwikserve rider films: prompts for an AI video tool

Two realistic 10-second clips. Upload `qwikserve-logo-reference.png` (in this folder) as the reference image for both.

## Settings

- Aspect ratio 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps, 10 seconds
- No music, no captions, no on-screen text
- Reference image: `qwikserve-logo-reference.png`
- For video 2, also give the tool the last frame of video 1 (or the same character reference) so it is the same rider

If the tool only makes shorter clips, generate each numbered shot separately and keep the wording of the rider, uniform and setting identical. The two shots can then be joined with a smooth transition and trimmed to 10 seconds.
