Our story.

Qwikserve began in Kolkata in August 2019 with one simple idea: delivery companies need riders they can count on, and the city is full of people looking for steady, honest work. We set out to bring the two together, and to stay with both long after the first shift.

We started small, from a head office at Kankaria Estate, recruiting riders for local hubs one conversation at a time. Our field recruiters met people in their own neighbourhoods; our telecallers followed up every enquiry. Before anyone reported for work, they knew the hub, the hours and who to call.

That habit of explaining the work first is what partners came to rely on. Hubs got riders who lived close enough to report on time and understood the shift before they arrived. Riders got someone who answered the phone when a day went wrong.

As the partners grew, so did the job. Recruitment became deployment, then day-to-day support on the ground. Today we also run whole delivery operations for some partners: rosters and attendance, team leaders at the hub, rider payouts, cash-on-delivery reconciliation and the EV fleet.

Along the way we noticed that many good riders were held back by one thing: not owning a bike. EV rental for riders working with us opened that door, and it put more electric two-wheelers on Kolkata’s roads. Our own EV brand is the next step.

More than a thousand riders have now onboarded with Qwikserve, across quick commerce, e-commerce, food, courier, retail, pharmacy and hyperlocal delivery. The idea we started with has not changed: Delivering Trust, Delivering Happiness.

OUR FOUNDER

Nilesh Kumar Singh.
Founder, Qwikserve.

Nilesh Kumar Singh founded Qwikserve Logistics in Kolkata in August 2019. He saw two needs that met in the same place: delivery companies needed riders they could rely on, and riders needed someone who would find them steady work and stand by them once it began.

He started with recruitment and built the company around everything that follows it: screening, onboarding, deployment, support after day one and, in time, running whole delivery operations. Under his leadership Qwikserve has onboarded more than 1,100 riders and worked with some of India’s best-known delivery platforms.

The idea he started with is still the one on our door: Delivering Trust, Delivering Happiness.

THE TEAM BEHIND THE TEAM

On the ground.
At the other end of the phone.

Six teams connect the journey, from the first enquiry to ongoing coordination at the hub.

Field recruiters

Meet prospective riders in their own neighbourhoods and explain the work, the reporting point and the vehicle options.

Telecallers

Follow up every enquiry by phone, answer first questions and set up the next conversation.

Back office

Keep documentation and onboarding records in order and answer riders’ account questions.

Operations coordinators

Match riders to hubs and shifts, and keep each reporting point informed as requirements change.

Rider support

A team riders can call about an assignment, a vehicle or an earnings question.

Team leaders

Stay close to riders on the ground and help sort out day-to-day issues during an assignment.

WHAT GUIDES US

Simple habits.

Start with the requirement
