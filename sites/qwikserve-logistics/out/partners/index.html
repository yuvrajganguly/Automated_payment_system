GOOD TO KNOW

Partner questions

Details to help prepare your staffing brief.

Can you support more than one location?

Share each location and its rider requirement. The team can assess the operating brief and discuss local availability with you.

Can we discuss bulk or ongoing recruitment?

Yes. Bulk and ongoing manpower support are part of Qwikserve’s offering. Share the expected numbers, reporting points and intended start dates.

What if some riders need a vehicle?

Tell us how many riders may need rental support. Only riders working under Qwikserve can rent through this arrangement. A separate rental company supplies the EVs; the coordinator checks local availability and terms.

How are rates and start dates confirmed?

After understanding the location, service and manpower requirement, the team can discuss availability, onboarding and commercial terms. An enquiry does not reserve riders.

Can Qwikserve run the whole delivery operation?

Yes. Beyond supplying riders, we can manage the day-to-day operation at your hub: rosters and attendance, team leaders on the ground, rider payouts, COD reconciliation and the EV fleet, with one point of contact and regular reporting.

Which kinds of delivery operations do you work with?

Quick commerce, e-commerce, food delivery, courier and express, retail, pharmaceutical, hyperlocal and on-demand delivery. Our operating experience includes work associated with platforms such as Spencer’s (Jiffy), Myntra, DealShare, Zomato, Shadowfax and Delhivery.

Where do you operate?

Kolkata is our head office and primary operations base. Share your exact reporting point and we will discuss local availability.

Who looks after riders once they are deployed?

Operations coordinators keep your reporting point informed, while rider support and team leaders stay in touch with riders during the assignment. You have one team to call rather than several.
