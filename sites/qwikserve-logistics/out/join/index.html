How to join Qwikserve.

  1. Give the recruiter your preferred area, when you can work and whether you have your own vehicle.

  2. Ask about the hub, shift timings and delivery work. Your recruiter will explain which local assignments are available.

  3. Check the documents you need. If you plan to rent an EV, discuss the supplier’s terms and local availability with your coordinator.

  4. Confirm the time, address and person to ask for. Keep the coordinator’s number handy for your first visit.

VEHICLE OPTIONS

No vehicle?
We’ve got you covered.

You don’t need your own bike to start. Riders without a vehicle can take a rented electric scooter through our rental partner, and many of our riders already do.

EV rental is available only to riders working under Qwikserve. A separate rental company supplies the vehicles. Your coordinator can check local availability and explain the rental terms before handover.

If you do have your own vehicle, tell the recruiter its type and your preferred reporting area when you enquire.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you join

Answers to common questions from prospective riders.

Can I enquire if I do not own a vehicle?

Yes. Tell the recruiter that you would like to discuss an EV rental option. Only riders working under Qwikserve can use this rental option. A separate company supplies the EVs, subject to local availability and agreed terms.

What documents should I bring?

Your recruiter will explain the documentation required for the assignment. Start with your name, contact number, area and vehicle situation; do not email identity or bank documents with your first enquiry.

Does sending my interest confirm a job?

No. The recruiter needs to discuss local openings, the assignment and onboarding with you before confirming next steps.

I already ride with Qwikserve. Where do I get help?

Use the Rider Support page for assignment, vehicle or earnings queries. You do not need to submit a new joining enquiry.

Where can I work?

Our main base is Kolkata. Tell the recruiter your preferred area and we will discuss local openings.

Who do I talk to after I join?

Your recruiter helps you get started. After that, the operations coordinator for your hub, rider support and your team leader are there for assignment, vehicle or earnings questions.
