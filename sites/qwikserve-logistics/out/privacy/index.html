WEBSITE INFORMATION

Your information.

This page explains how the enquiry tools on this version of the Qwikserve website work.

How enquiries are sent

When you submit a form, your details are sent securely to Qwikserve’s inbox at info@qwikserve.in through FormSubmit, an email-relay service. The website itself does not keep a database of enquiries or create an application or support ticket.

If the form cannot be sent, it offers to send the same message on WhatsApp, through Gmail or your email app, or to copy it. WhatsApp, FormSubmit and your email service handle messages under their own terms and privacy settings.

Information to include

Business enquiries use your contact and operational requirements. Joining enquiries use basic contact, area and vehicle information. Support enquiries use enough context for the team to understand your question.

Do not include bank details, Aadhaar, identity-document scans or other sensitive records in these initial forms. The team can explain the appropriate verification or follow-up channel.

Your choices

You can edit the form before sending, leave without sending, or use a phone contact instead. The site does not save form entries to local browser storage. The rider figures on the Home and Highlights pages are read from an aggregate count that contains no personal details.

Website services

This build does not add advertising trackers or an analytics service. The hosting service and your browser may process technical connection information to load the website. External directions and email links take you to other services.

Questions or requests

For a question about information you have sent to Qwikserve, contact info@qwikserve.in or call the team.
